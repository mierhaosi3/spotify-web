title: Notes from a pixel garden
date: 2026-07-18
summary: Why a personal site should feel like weather, not a dashboard.
---

A homepage can be a room. Plants on the sides, music in the middle, rain when the sky says so.

I wanted the first viewport to stay one composition — not a wall of cards. Resume points expand in place; long writing leaves the room through a quiet door.

The garden stays. The weather changes.
